# What this is not

- Not financial, legal, medical or political advice: the video describes and analyses.
- Not a rate for other work. One task, one rule and one made-up library, given to three Claude models in one version
  of Claude Code on one day, ten runs each way: the counts say what happened here, not how often it happens elsewhere
  or with other vendors' agents.
- Not a security evaluation of Claude Code's hooks, or of any sandbox. No run was told to get past a guard, and the
  shell hook's patterns were tested on six commands, not against an agent set on writing.
- The close's advice ("Write the rule down", "enforce the effect, not a tool") is argued from this one task, and is
  marked "argued" on screen; it was not measured anywhere else.
- The verification readiness number measures how complete and checked the evidence is, not whether the video is right.
- A model's check of a source is a development signal, not authority. `CHECKED.md` says who checked what.
